DEFERRAL RULE — applies to ALL worker decisions, not just review findings.
Default: fold into this PR; the user prefers larger PRs that mix concerns
a little over a trail of follow-up issues. Defer only when ALL hold:
  1. The work is NOT requested or implied by the current issue body.
  2. EITHER the issue body explicitly flags it as a design decision for
     the user, OR the issue-lead has told you (in lead_guidance) that
     this specific work is out of scope.
  3. It would more than double the PR diff AND needs distinct expertise,
     new infrastructure, multi-day experiments, or a clearly unrelated
     module. Size alone is not enough: a 400-line addition to files you
     are already editing still gets folded in.
When in doubt, do not defer; bring it to the issue-lead with your reasoning.
